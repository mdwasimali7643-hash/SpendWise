Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Klaro Tech ("we", "us", or "our") provides SpendWISE, a personal finance tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use SpendWISE.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, SpendWISE may process:

  • Account information: Email address and authentication identifiers associated with your account.
  • Financial records: Income, expenses, transaction descriptions, categories, dates, budgets, and savings goals you enter.
  • AI Adviser messages: Questions and relevant context submitted to the AI Adviser to generate responses.
  • Technical information: Limited technical information processed by the app or service providers to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

Avoid entering passwords, bank login credentials, full payment card numbers, or other highly sensitive information into transaction descriptions or AI Adviser messages.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide account sign-in and authentication.
  • Save and display transactions, budgets, and savings goals.
  • Calculate balances, summaries, and spending analytics.
  • Provide AI Adviser responses when you use that feature.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve the app.
  • Respond to support requests and applicable legal obligations.

3. Storage and Service Providers

SpendWISE uses Google Firebase services for features such as authentication and cloud data storage. Information processed by these services is subject to applicable Google and Firebase terms and privacy documentation.

If you use the AI Adviser, the text you submit and relevant context included with your request may be sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. Service providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, protect their systems, and comply with applicable requirements.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above or disclosed when necessary to comply with the law, protect the service, or address security and abuse issues.

5. Security

We rely on technical and organizational measures, including security features offered by our service providers, to help protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Advertising and Analytics

This policy does not claim that advertising or analytics services are enabled. If such services are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe the relevant providers and information practices.

7. Account and Data Deletion

SpendWISE provides an in-app option to request deletion of your account. Use the account deletion option in the app and follow the confirmation steps displayed.

When account deletion is completed successfully, the app is intended to remove the associated Firebase Authentication account and the financial records linked to that account in Firebase, including applicable transactions, budgets, and savings goals.

Deletion may be irreversible. Make sure you no longer need your records before confirming. Do not assume that uninstalling the app deletes your account or cloud-stored data.

Some information may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or records, or may need to be retained where required by law. The timing and scope of such removal depend on the relevant services and applicable requirements.

If you cannot access the in-app deletion option or have a question about a deletion request, contact klarotech.official@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SpendWISE is designed to be approachable for a broad audience, including young users. Use of the app should comply with applicable age requirements and laws. If you have a privacy concern about information provided by a child, contact us so we can review it.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. You can use the in-app account deletion option or contact us to ask about available options.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as SpendWISE changes or as required by law. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
