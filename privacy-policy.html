Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Klaro Tech ("we", "us", or "our") provides SpendWISE, a personal finance tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use SpendWISE.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, SpendWISE may process:

  • Account information: Information associated with your account, such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Financial records: Income, expenses, transaction descriptions, categories, dates, and budget information you enter.
  • AI Adviser messages: Questions and other text you submit to the AI Adviser, together with information needed to generate a response.
  • Technical information: Limited technical data that may be processed by the app or its service providers to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

Please avoid entering passwords, bank login credentials, full payment card numbers, or other highly sensitive information into transaction descriptions or AI Adviser messages.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide account sign-in and authentication.
  • Save and display your transactions and budgets.
  • Calculate balances, summaries, and spending analytics.
  • Provide AI Adviser responses when you use that feature.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve the app.
  • Respond to support requests and applicable legal obligations.

3. Storage and Service Providers

SpendWISE uses Google Firebase services for features such as authentication and cloud data storage. Information handled by these services is subject to the applicable Google and Firebase terms and privacy documentation.

If you use the AI Adviser, the text you submit and any relevant context included with your request may be sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. Do not include confidential information in AI requests.

Service providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, protect their systems, and comply with applicable requirements. Their own terms and policies may also apply.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, or disclosed when necessary to protect the service, comply with the law, or address security and abuse issues.

5. Security

We rely on appropriate technical and organizational measures, including security features offered by our service providers, to help protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for using a device you trust.

6. Advertising and Analytics

This policy does not claim that advertising or analytics services are currently enabled. If we introduce advertising or analytics providers in a future version, we will update this policy to describe the relevant services and information practices before relying on this section as a description of those practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information and financial records may remain in the cloud services used by SpendWISE while they are associated with your account.

The current version of SpendWISE does not include an in-app option to delete an account or all associated financial data. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete cloud-stored information.

To ask about deleting your account or associated information, contact us at klarotech.official@gmail.com . We will review your request and explain the steps available. Requests may require verification that you own the account.

Any deletion request will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements and the capabilities of the services involved. Information may remain in backups or provider records for a limited period where applicable.

Do not assume that sending a request or uninstalling the app automatically completes deletion. We will communicate the available process when you contact us.

8. Children's Privacy

SpendWISE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us so we can review the matter.
